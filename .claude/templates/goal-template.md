# [Goal Name]

**Structure:** `[Verb] + [specific deliverable/change] + [quantified target]` in SMART format.
Include the Done-When Statement.

**Examples:**
- "Cut time-to-first-answer in self-serve help to under 30 seconds on every surface"
- "Launch an automated refunds workflow that self-resolves X% of refund tickets at ≥ X% CSAT"
- "Rebuild, retire, or refactor the 3 lowest-success onboarding flows to lift completion to ≥ 80%"

---

## What is the outcome?

*Formula:* `[Who] will be able to [do what] / experience [what change], measured by [metric crossing threshold]`

*Example:* "New mobile users will reach a working answer in ≤ 2 taps from any screen, measured by resolution rate ≥ 90% and engagement ≥ 60%."

---

## So what? Why should we do this?

*Formula:* `Without this, [cost / risk / missed opportunity]. With this, [unlock / lever / compounding effect].`

*Example:* "Without this, mobile resolution keeps drifting and we lose new users in their first week. With this, we close the desktop–mobile gap and set up the next wave of automation on a solid base."

---

## Why now?

*Formula:* `[Forcing function] lands this cycle, which means [if we don't act] / [cost of delay is]. The unlock window is [dates].`

*Example:* "The new platform foundation ships this half. If mobile parity isn't in place by end of Q3, the rollout starts on a fractured base and we spend Q4 retrofitting. The window closes in early September."
